The Verb To Be

Introduce yourself. Describe people. Express how you feel.

V.
THE CORE IDEA

The verb to be helps us describe identity, feelings and states. Its form changes depending on the subject.

SENTENCE DECONSTRUCTION EX. 001

I SUBJECT am VERB a student. COMPLEMENT

WHO? WHAT CONNECTS? WHAT INFORMATION?
!

Choose the correct form

I → AM I am ready.
HE / SHE / IT → IS She is happy.
YOU / WE / THEY → ARE They are here.
